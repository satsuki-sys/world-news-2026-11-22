One image summing up the world news of **Sunday 22 November 2026**, drawn
as a newspaper front page by `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only).
It writes `/output/entry.png`: a 1024×1024 PNG, deterministic across runs.

## What the image shows

- **Lead — G20 opens in Africa for the first time.** The summit opens in
  Johannesburg under South Africa's presidency; the United States boycotts
  it and the leaders adopt a declaration.
- **Gulf war — Bahrain.** Iran claims missile and drone strikes on US
  Patriot radars at the al-Riffa and Awali bases.
- **Mexico.** Forces capture cartel kingpin Fausto Isidro Meza Flores,
  "El Chapo Isidro"; 11 people are killed in the violence that follows.
- **Peru.** A magnitude 6.7 earthquake shakes the Andes; no deaths reported.
- **Washington.** The Pentagon moves to curb US–Canada defence ties.
- **The Hague.** The world court finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against
  the Rohingya; Rohingya survivors hail the verdict.
- **Also today:** Trump vows a 10% cap on credit-card rates; Bitcoin
  rebounds 8% to near $60,000; Verstappen takes pole in Las Vegas;
  Australia edge England in a T20 thriller at the MCG; Japan and India
  agree live-fire drills; TikTok launches a deepfake detector.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
